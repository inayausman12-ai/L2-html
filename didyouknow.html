Fact 1: The Amazing Octopus

Octopuses have three hearts, nine brains, and blue blood

How smart are octopuses?

Octopuses can open jars, solve puzzles and even recognise human faces.

Fact 2: Lightning Strikes

Lightning strikes the Earth about 100 times every single second.

How hot is lightning?

A single bolt of lightning is around 30,000 Kelvin

Can lightning strike twice?

Yes! Lightning absolutely can and does strike the same place more than once.

Fact 3: The Deep Ocean

More than 80% of the world's oceans remain unexplored.

How deep does the ocean go?

The deepest point on Earth is Mariana Trench, which reaches a depth of about 11 kilometres.

Fun Comparison

If you placed Mount Everest at the bottom of the Mariana Trech, its peak would still be more than 2 kilometres underwater.
